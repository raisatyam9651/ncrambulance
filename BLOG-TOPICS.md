# Emergency First-Aid Blog: Question List & Content Plan

**Theme:** "What can an ordinary person do to save a life until the ambulance arrives?"
**Audience:** Families, bystanders, office staff and drivers in Delhi NCR. None of them have medical training.
**URL pattern:** `/blog/<slug>` (each post is a single file `blog/<slug>.php`)
**Internal-link targets:** homepage services (`/#services`, `/#types`), booking (`/#book`), `/contact` and the location pages (`/ambulance-service/<city>/`). All service URLs live in one map in `blog/_inc/blog.php` (`link_to()`). When dedicated service pages are built, change the map once and every blog link updates.

Each post answers one **main question** (the H1/search intent). Its **sub-questions** become H2 sections and FAQs.

---
