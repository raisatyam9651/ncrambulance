Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

Important Disclaimer: We are NOT a direct ambulance provider. We operate as an emergency connection and dispatch service. When you call our helpline or book through our website, we immediately transfer your request and connect you with the nearest independent ambulance provider in your area.

1. Introduction

Welcome to our Ambulance Service connection platform. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website or contact our helpline.

2. Service Clarification

As clearly stated above, we are a dispatch and connection service. We do not own or operate our own fleet of ambulances. Our primary role is to rapidly connect you with third-party ambulance operators situated nearest to your location to ensure quick response times.

3. Information We Collect

When you book an ambulance through our platform, we may collect the following information:

  • Patient Name
  • Mobile Number
  • Pickup Location (Address, area, or hospital)
  • Drop Location
  • Type of ambulance required

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for the purpose of facilitating your emergency request. We will share your provided name, contact number, and location details with the nearest independent ambulance provider so they can reach you as quickly as possible. We do not sell or rent your personal information to marketing agencies.

5. Data Security

We take appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the contact methods listed on our homepage.
